**Back2Life** is a personal Android app for tracking rehabilitation exercises assigned by your own
therapist, along with pain, symptoms and rest during the day.

This repository hosts the app's **Privacy Policy** as a public page linked from its Google Play
listing. The app's source code is not published here.

- 📄 **Privacy Policy:** https://renedva.github.io/Back2Life/
- 📱 **Google Play:** [added on release]
- ✉️ **Contact:** [contact email]

### Policy highlights
- Your data stays **on your device**.
- **Optional** backup to a folder in **your own** Google Drive only, using the limited `drive.file` scope.
- No server, no ads, no analytics, no data sharing with third parties.
- Full in-app deletion of all your data at any time.
